# FX7a - New effects from Shadertoy-class sources, GLSL libraries and shader archives (27 Oct 2026)

User (27 Oct): "research more and build more after all this". This file lists **35 real-time effects people build that
the repo does not have yet** (checked against `FX0_effects_backlog.md` rows 1-82, `RUBENFRO_COVERAGE.md`, the live
`builders/` list and the `docs/builds/*.md` module tables). Offline research only: TD, Arena, REST, OSC and Syphon
were not touched, nothing was cloned or downloaded into the project, nothing was compiled.

**Nothing here is measured.** Every ms is my estimate at 1080x1920 on the M1 Max under Rosetta / MoltenVK (TD
2025.31550) and must be replaced by `td_perf` + real fps at build time (60 fps target, ~3 ms per COMP). Multi-pass
modules are compute-mode GLSL (T-46 / T-47); single-pass ones can be a normal GLSL TOP. TD op and par names were not
re-read here; use `ops/ops_*_2025.31550.json` when building.

## 3. Top 10 for live shows (★)

1. **SX-01 Liquid glass panel** - modern look, panels over any visual (names, lyrics, UI); 0.3-0.6 ms.
