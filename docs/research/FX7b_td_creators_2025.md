# FX7b - What TouchDesigner creators built in 2025-26: 40 effects and techniques the lab does not have (27 Oct 2026)

User ask (27 Oct): "research more and build more", "see what people are building and add to it". This is the TD-creator lane
of the 27 Oct research round (siblings: `FX7a_shadertoy_glsl_libraries.md`, `FX7c_other_tools_effects.md`,
`FX7d_scan_artists.md`). Research only: TouchDesigner, Arena, Resolume were not touched; nothing was installed, downloaded
into the project, loaded or run. Target build is **TD 2025.31550** (macOS, Rosetta/x86_64, Vulkan/MoltenVK), output
**1080x1920**, budget ~3 ms per COMP at 60 fps (`CLAUDE.md`).

## 5. Trends (what the creator scene did in 2025-26)

POPs took over: since the 2025 release the monthly output on derivative.ca and YouTube is dominated by POP tutorials
(Particle POP + Math Mix POP + Lookup Texture POP, then Proximity, Trail, Skin, Ray and the GLSL POP family), and the
visible shift is from "instance a sphere" to **attribute thinking** (a custom attribute drives a ramp, a ramp drives a
transition). Creators now sell small dependable operators rather than big projects: C++ custom operator families (MCX,
Spiraldiver's FOP/STOP, Alaghast's Voronoi/L-System POPs, TDFam with 12 families from 8 creators, POPX) and Patreon-gated
.toe files, so most "free" tutorials teach the idea and hide the file. Three look trends repeat: **tracking/HUD/plexus**
(blob tracking again, now unlimited via POPs), **cheap fakes over heavy simulation** (fake fluid, feedback sort-streaks,
2D molten chrome), and **single-shader worlds** (Yaado Lab's planet and dream corridor; BS_ already covers the maths).
Mac-native ML arrived through *plugins and bridges* (Apple Vision Suite, CoreML bridges, Kinect 360/v2 on arm64) which is
good news for features we lack (optical flow) but collides with our Rosetta x86_64 TD unless the model runs out of process.
Music-sync is mostly simple: kick/snare toggles one or two things (instant infinite's "80/20 rule": kick -> invert
threshold, snare -> gap size, MIDI for the rest), and elekktronaut's AVY (24 Oct 2026; presets, MIDI map, fx, signal
modules wired by cables with ranges, Link, OSC; written with Cursor over 6-8 months) is the freshest peer to our rack idea.
Lighting is converging with visuals (DMX POPs, PosiStageNet, Particle-POP logic into fixtures). AI shows up as tooling
(Embody/Envoy MCP, TD skills for Claude Code, Derivative's TDMCP beta, SD TOP) rather than as an effect we need to copy.
Heavy Windows/CUDA offers (ClothPOP, Harlow path tracer, Box3D, Object Detection POP, SD TOP) are not usable on this Mac.
