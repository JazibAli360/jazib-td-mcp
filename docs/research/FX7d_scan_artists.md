## 0. Findings

1. **The splat-native scene has moved on from "point FX" to a lighting problem.** The 2025-26 tools that matter to a stage workflow (Houdini 22 +
   SideFX Labs, GSOPs, WallGS Pro, Volinga Pro, Splatlight, Notch 2026.2, Arnold 7.5) all converge on the same list: **delight, normals,
   relight with a baked-vs-lit blend, shadows from a proxy mesh or from splat opacity, emissive splats that light others, and a depth pass**.
   RUBENFRO_COVERAGE has L1 (lights) but with "shadows: none in v1" and no delight / normals / bounce light / depth. SA1-SA6, SA13 fill that.
2. **Real production uses of splats (Helicopter, Superman holograms, NouerA .exe) treat splats as relit, glitched performance footage**, not as
   pretty clouds: packet glitches that reassemble on the dialogue (SA18), 2D temporal denoise because per-frame training flickers (SA17),
   skeleton-driven collisions (SA29), opacity pushed up so a face does not show the back of the head (already BL `Opacitygain`).
3. **Weather and reveal tools from the web scene (3DGS Weather, Oshima Splat Dissolve, Spark effects) are cheap to copy**: all of them are per-splat
   maths on position / scale / colour plus a hidden collision mesh for depth. Nothing needs a new engine.
4. **A shared 3D voxel grid** (coarse, 32^3 to 128^3, in the effectors COMP) is used by six rows below (SA1, SA3, SA5, SA14, SA15, SA21) and by W3-B's
   distance field (K36). That is the "2+ real modules use it now" case of the no-bloat rule: build it once, as a service, not per module.
5. **Gaps in this lane (honest):** geronimo giqueaux and sur.render have no public technique text beyond the videos BR already read (a search
   found nothing new); Refik Anadol's pages say "LiDAR / photogrammetry as training data" and nothing about a point or splat renderer; Marshmallow
   Laser Feast is 2018-19 work (vvvv, 800 M-point decimation) so out of the 2023-26 window; Kyle McDonald's scan work is 2011-12 structured light.
   Pages that returned 403 / 404 or unreadable text: Union VFX (cgw.com), ArtStation Timbuktu video (snippet only), Notch manual node page (127 k chars,
   not read), one Reddit thread (404). Nothing was inferred from them beyond the snippet.

## 3. Top 10 (marked ★ in the tables) and build order

Ranked by (user rule: lights, geometry, haze, glow, colour affect the splats) x (value for a live LED show) / (cost):
1. **SA4 proxy-mesh occluders / shadows** - hides rain, stops beams at walls, shadows without splat-on-splat cost; reuses `px_geo` and the haze march. S-M.
2. **SA13 splat depth pass** - enabler: edges (G2), haze occlusion, geometry softness, DOF, slab. M.
3. **SA5 emissive bounce** - the burning figure and orbs light the alley (Ruben's own look, now genuinely lit). M, needs the shared grid.
4. **SA8 flares the scan can block** - makes placed lights believable for ~0.1 ms. S.
5. **SA3 self-shadowing from opacity** - the second shadow route for lights inside the scan. M-L (after SA4).
6. **SA1 delight (+ SA2 normals)** - makes `light.relight` look right instead of double-lit. S-M (load pass).
7. **SA6 screen-as-light** - the scan lit by the wall content. S.
8. **SA18 rigid packet glitch** - production-proven look (Superman), one extension to `glitch`. S.
9. **SA21 heat / mask diffusion** - fire that spreads over surfaces; uses the grid. S-M.
10. **SA23 persistent painted mask volume** - the interactive-installation hook (hands paint a reveal that stays). M.

Suggested order: shared voxel grid service (SA1, SA3, SA5, SA14, SA21, SA23) -> SA4 -> SA8 -> SA13 -> SA5 -> SA1 + SA2 -> SA6 -> SA3 -> SA18 -> SA21 -> SA23.
Everything else (SA7, SA9-SA12, SA14-SA17, SA19-SA20, SA22, SA24-SA35) is a tail. A conflict check against live work: SA2 and SA3 touch `effectors.md`
(W1-A owner), SA4 / SA9 / SA13 touch `px_geo` (W1-E owner), SA8 / SA13 touch `px_post` / `px_haze` (W1-D), SA11 / SA12 / SA16 touch the PX core
